## Original goals and test thresholds
Student's own words, preserved from the Sprint 3 kickoff (2026-10-15), one question at a time.

**1. What decision or challenge am I addressing? What remains uncertain?**
Partially coming up with features that will actually be helpful to a coach and is a big enough problem. Because coaches have been doing practice tracking and team management manually. I am worried it is more of a vitamin rather than a pill.

**2. Who can I reach, and what do they do now instead?**
I can talk to my coworkers. All of them write their own practices and [not an assumption, per her own correction] they either don't do anything to track swimmers goals and take attendance or they make excel spreadsheets by hand.

**3. What is the smallest observable test I can run?**
I want to show my project to one of my coworkers who is a coach and get feedback from them.

**4. What result would make me persist, pivot, or stop?**
Persist: if the coach told me they would use it or liked some of the potential features. (I would definitely use an AI generated practice myself, but that's because when I coach I'm usually just filling in for someone else — I need to see what an actual primary coach would do.)
Pivot or stop: if they don't see any coaches actually using this software.

**5. Where will AI help, and what will I personally verify or learn to explain?**
AI will help me build features with Claude Code, as well as help brainstorm with me after feedback on my demos. I don't ever just trust the AI's output — I check everything and prompt my AI to come up with what I want.

**6. What will count as meaningful progress toward my longer-term goal?**
I want to work out 3 of the main pain points for coaches and build them out in my website!
